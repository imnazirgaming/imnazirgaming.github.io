FinMex

FinMex is a free Android app that combines two personal trackers in one place:

FINEX – personal finance

  • Record income and expenses across multiple wallets
  • Track loans, pending bills and bill reminders
  • View charts and export financial statements as PDF

MOTEX – vehicle maintenance

  • Track fuel entries and mileage
  • Schedule maintenance reminders and keep service history
  • Store registration, tax and license documents for each vehicle

Optional Google Drive backup

You can sign in with Google to back up and sync your FINEX and MOTEX data. The app saves its backup in a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive (the "Application Data" folder). The app cannot see any of your other Drive files, and the developer cannot access your data.
