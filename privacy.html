Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FinMex Android app (including its FINEX finance tracker and MOTEX vehicle tracker, together "the app") handles your information. The app is developed by Imran Nazir ("the developer").

1. Data stored on your device

The information you enter into the app, such as transactions, wallets, categories, bills, to-do items, vehicle details, fuel and maintenance records, and photos or documents you attach, is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this data and does not run any server that collects it.

2. Sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, the app receives your Google account name, email address and profile picture. These are shown inside the app so you can see which account is connected. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.

3. Google Drive backup and sync (optional)

If you choose to sync, the app requests one Google Drive permission: access to its own Application Data folder (drive.appdata). This is a hidden folder that belongs only to this app in your Google Drive.

  • The app stores backup files of your FINEX and MOTEX data in that folder so you can restore or sync them on your devices.
  • The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • The backup files are stored in your own Google account. The developer cannot access them.
  • The app uses this data only to provide backup and sync. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose, and it is not sold or shared.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. No ads, no tracking

The app does not contain advertising and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

5. Deleting your data

  • Cloud backup: use the "Delete Google Drive data" button in the app, or remove the app's access in your Google Account settings under Security → Third-party access.
  • Local data: uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
